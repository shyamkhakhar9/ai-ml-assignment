# Visit Singapore: Essential Travel Information

Source: https://www.visitsingapore.com/travel-guide-tips/traveller-information/

Prepare for a visit to Singapore with practical visitor information from Visit Singapore.

## Travel Tips Before Visiting

### Weather and timezone

Enjoy summer all year-round with Singapore's tropical climate (25-33ºC). Dress in light, airy clothing and bring along an umbrella in case of occasional rainfall. Singapore Standard Time is GMT+8.

### Visa and entry requirements

While the majority of visitors do not require a visa, check the latest requirements and advisories for the most accurate information before travel.

### Smoking

Smoking is prohibited in buildings, retail food establishments, transport nodes, public service vehicles, and sports facilities. Designated smoking areas are marked with notices or signs.

### E-vaporisers

The purchase, possession or use of e-vaporisers, including e-vaporiser products, is illegal in Singapore. Visitors are strongly advised against bringing these items into the country.

### Safe drinking water

Tap water in Singapore surpasses World Health Organization standards. You can drink water from the tap or purchase bottled water from stores.

### Power plug

The standard electrical current is 220-240 volts AC (50 cycles). Power plugs use three square prongs.

### Language

You can communicate in English with Singaporeans, who are mostly fluent in it. Many also speak Mandarin Chinese, Malay or Tamil. You may also encounter Singlish, a colloquial form of English blended with local languages and dialects.

### Useful numbers

- Police: 999
- Ambulance and Fire Brigade: 995
- Flight information: 1800 542 4422

### Prayer facilities

Singapore is a Muslim-friendly destination with prayer facilities available at major hotels and attractions, and mosques located around the city.

### Wi-Fi

Visitors can access free Wi-Fi at Wireless@SGx hotspots with a foreign mobile number by downloading the Wireless@SGx app. Overseas charges may apply.

### Singapore Visitor Centres

Visitor Centres can help with itinerary planning, attraction tips, sightseeing tickets, accommodation, and souvenirs.

### Public dining places

It is an offence to leave trays and litter on tables at hawker centres, food courts and coffeeshops. Return trays and crockery at return stations.

## Money and payments

### Payment methods

Credit cards, Apple Pay, Samsung Pay, contactless payment and other tap-and-go options are widely accepted.

### Tipping

Tipping is not customary. Visitors who receive good service may still choose to tip.

### Tax refund

Tourists can claim a refund on the 9% Goods and Services Tax (GST) paid on purchases if they spend more than $100 at participating shops.

## Getting around Singapore

### Public transport

Singapore's public transport system is fast and efficient. The MRT (Mass Rapid Transit) and bus systems have an extensive network of routes. Plan journeys with the OneMap app for accessible routes and nearby amenities.

### Ways to pay

Public transport can be paid with contactless bank cards, mobile wallets, concession cards, EZ-Link cards, cash, or a Singapore Tourist Pass for all-day unlimited travel.

### Accessibility

All MRT stations and public buses are wheelchair accessible and open strollers are allowed. Approach train staff or bus captains for assistance.

### MRT map and buses

Download the MyTransport app for public transport routes and timings, or a Singapore MRT map. Public buses operate from as early as 5:30am until around 12:30 midnight and reach both popular attractions and quieter areas.

### Taxi or private hire

Taxis can be flagged on the street or at taxi stands, or booked by app. Taxis are metered and surcharges may vary with timing, route and service. Ride-hailing options include ComfortDelGro, Grab, Gojek, Ryde and TADA.
