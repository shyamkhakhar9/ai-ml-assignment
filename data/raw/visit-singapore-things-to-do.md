# Visit Singapore: Things to Do

Canonical source: https://www.visitsingapore.com/see-do-singapore/

Attractions, neighbourhoods, indoor conservatories, museums, and outdoor parks recommended by Visit Singapore.

## Gardens by the Bay

Source: https://www.visitsingapore.com/neighbourhood/featured-neighbourhood/marina-bay/gardens-by-the-bay/

### Indoor: Flower Dome and Cloud Forest

The Flower Dome in Bay South Gardens covers 1.28 hectares and displays plants and flowers from five continents. It has been recognised as the world's largest glass greenhouse. From the Flower Dome, walk to the Cloud Forest to see one of the world's tallest indoor waterfalls. These conservatories are strong indoor options during rain.

### Outdoor: Supertrees and art

Supertrees are vertical gardens that light up after dusk. The OCBC Skyway is an aerial walkway between Supertrees. The structures collect rainwater, generate solar power, and vent the conservatories. Garden Rhapsody is a daily light and sound show. The gardens also display more than 200 sculptures.

## Chinatown

Source: https://www.visitsingapore.com/neighbourhood/featured-neighbourhood/chinatown/

Chinatown mixes heritage and contemporary energy. Highlights include:

- Chinatown Heritage Centre, presenting stories of early shophouse residents.
- Smith Street shops, eateries, and cultural activity.
- Thian Hock Keng Temple, among Singapore's oldest Chinese temples.
- Chinatown Street Market for souvenirs.
- Chinatown Complex, a large hawker centre including Michelin Bib Gourmand stalls.
- Maxwell Food Centre, known for hawker dishes such as chicken rice.

Nearby cultural neighbourhoods include Little India.

## Buddha Tooth Relic Temple and Museum

Source: https://www.visitsingapore.com/neighbourhood/featured-neighbourhood/chinatown/buddha-tooth-relic-temple/

Built in 2007 in a Tang-inspired style, the temple houses a sacred relic in a large gold stupa and includes two museums. This is an indoor cultural visit suitable for families interested in heritage.

## Peranakan Museum

Source: https://www.visitsingapore.com/neighbourhood/featured-neighbourhood/civic-district/peranakan-museum/

An indoor museum of Peranakan (Straits-born) heritage, covering Chinese, Malay, and Indonesian influences through clothing, beadwork, and domestic artefacts. It occupies the former Tao Nan School (1912).

## Neighbourhoods for culture

Visit Singapore features neighbourhoods including Chinatown, Civic District, Dempsey, Kampong Gelam, Katong-Joo Chiat, Little India, Mandai, Marina Bay, Orchard Road, Sentosa Island, and the Singapore River.

Kampong Gelam and Haji Lane are known for Malay heritage, independent shops, and food. Little India combines traditional trades, architecture, garments, and jewellery.

## Family fun, nature, and indoor options

Source: https://www.visitsingapore.com/things-to-do/top-things-to-do/family-fun/

Visit Singapore groups things to do into unique experiences, city in nature, culture and heritage, iconic architecture, family fun, after dark, museums and galleries, shopping, and dining.

Indoor-friendly ideas include Flower Dome, Cloud Forest, museums and galleries, and temples. Outdoor ideas include Supertrees and OCBC Skyway, Singapore Botanic Gardens, neighbourhood walking, and Mandai wildlife parks.

Green spaces and nature parks are listed under urban wellness on Visit Singapore, including parks suitable for walking when weather is dry.

## Food experiences

Visit Singapore dining categories include local food and drinks, hawker centres, halal food, and vegetarian and vegan options. Chinatown Complex and Maxwell Food Centre are practical starting points for hawker food.
