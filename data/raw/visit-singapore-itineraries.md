## 4 Days in Singapore

Source: https://www.visitsingapore.com/travel-tips/travelling-to-singapore/itineraries/4-days-in-singapore/

In town for a while? Shop, eat, and play with this detailed itinerary.

### Day 1: Explore the city

Start in Kampong Gelam, a conserved area where Malay royalty once resided, steeped in culture and history. Have a Singapore-style breakfast at Zam Zam Restaurant with prata (South Indian flatbread) and curry. Continue to Haji Lane, a street of indie outlets, vintage clothing, and lifestyle shops such as Hygge.

### Day 2: Visit neighbourhoods

Explore Katong-Joo Chiat in the East, known for rainbow-hued heritage shophouses, cafes, and boutiques. Have an old-school breakfast at Chin Mee Chin Confectionery with buns and coconut kaya jam. Visit Kim Choo Kueh Chang for Peranakan wares, souvenirs, and cultural products.

### Day 3: Nature (and a spot of shopping)

Spend time at the Singapore Botanic Gardens, a 150-year-old green destination and Singapore's first UNESCO World Heritage Site. Do not miss the National Orchid Garden, with a very large orchid display (over 60,000 plants and orchids). Brunch at The Halia in the Ginger Garden, which serves contemporary European cuisine.

### Day 4: Wildlife

Head north to Mandai for world-class wildlife attractions. Bird Paradise is home to over 3,500 birds in walk-through aviaries. Alternatively visit Singapore Zoo, with more than 2,400 animals across 300 species, including free-roaming animal habitats.
